Help Center

Trouble registering?
Double-check your WhatsApp number is entered without the leading zero, and that you've selected the correct country code. Each number can only register once.

Didn't receive a WhatsApp confirmation?
Make sure the number you registered is active and able to receive messages. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, contact us below.

Want to change your category?
Reach out to us on WhatsApp with your registered number and the category you'd like instead.

Want to delete your data?
Contact us and we'll process your deletion request — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Still need help?

Reach us on WhatsApp or by email — see the Contact Us section in the footer below.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join S.K Community?
Fill in the registration form on this page with your name, WhatsApp number and details, then submit. You'll get a confirmation message on WhatsApp right after.

Do I need to pay anything to register?
No. Registration is free.

Can I register more than once?
No. One registration per person — duplicate accounts are removed.

What happens after I register?
You're added to the community and may be contacted about brand campaigns, job opportunities, and giveaways matched to your category.

Is my WhatsApp number required?
Yes, and it must be active since that's how we send your confirmation and any opportunities.

How is my data used?
Your details are used only to connect you with opportunities and are not sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I delete my data or stop messages?
Yes — you can request deletion at any time, and you can reply STOP to any WhatsApp message to opt out.

About S.K Community

S.K Community is building Ghana and Africa's biggest WhatsApp community — connecting brands directly to customers, creating content jobs, and funding businesses.

Our Goals

  • Build the biggest WhatsApp contact network in Ghana and across Africa
  • Create real content jobs and paid opportunities for registered members
  • Connect brands directly to their target audience without middlemen

Who can join

Anyone with a WhatsApp number — influencers, content creators, students, entrepreneurs, and anyone who wants to be first in line for opportunities.

Community Rules
  1. Use your real name — fake names will be removed
  2. The WhatsApp number you register must be active and saved on your phone
  3. One registration per person — duplicate accounts will be deleted
  4. No spamming or sharing other members' contact details
  5. Treat all brand communications with respect
  6. To opt out of messages at any time, reply STOP to any WhatsApp message
  7. SK Brand reserves the right to remove any member who violates these rules
How It Works

1. Fill Your Details
Complete the registration form with your personal information, social media profile and your category.

2. Get Your Number
Receive your unique contact number and a brand message on WhatsApp immediately after registration.

3. Join Campaigns
We reach out directly with targeted campaigns matched to your category, country and audience size.

Privacy Policy

S.K Community collects your name, WhatsApp number, country, and social platform details when you register. This information is used to connect you with brands, job opportunities, and giveaways.

What we collect

Name, WhatsApp number, country, social media platform, follower range, and referral source.

How we use it

We send you job alerts, giveaway announcements, and brand campaign invitations via WhatsApp. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time. To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any WhatsApp message.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us on WhatsApp or through the deletion request form.
